Data We Collect

Wearcast processes photos you select for the virtual try-on feature. These photos are only sent to Replicate (replicate.com) for AI processing and are not stored on your device.

How Your Photos Are Used

Your selected clothing and person photos are sent to Replicate servers so the AI model can virtually dress you. The image is deleted by Replicate after processing. Your photos are not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the Replicate API for AI image processing. You can read Replicate's privacy policy at replicate.com/privacy.

Credit System

Credits you purchase are stored locally on your device. Payments are processed via the Apple App Store and subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at users under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from this age group.

Contact

For questions about our privacy policy: destek@tryiton.app
