What is Wearcast?

Wearcast is an AI virtual try-on app. Add a clothing photo and a photo of yourself, and the app shows you how the outfit looks on you in seconds.

How do I try on an outfit?

Open the Discover tab, tap "Try your own" (or pick a look), add a clothing photo and a portrait photo, then tap "Try On". Your result appears in about 20–40 seconds. You can save or share it.

How do credits work?

Each try-on uses credits. You can buy credit packs, or subscribe to Premium for monthly credits and priority processing. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID: open the App Store app, tap your profile, then Subscriptions. You can cancel anytime; the free trial can be cancelled before it ends to avoid charges.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the purchase screen in the app and tap "Restore" (top-left) to restore an active subscription on a new device.

Privacy

Your photos are used only for the AI try-on and are not stored. Read our full Privacy Policy.
