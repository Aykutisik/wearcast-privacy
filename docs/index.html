Data We Collect

Wearcast processes photos you select for the virtual try-on feature. These photos are only sent to Replicate (replicate.com) for AI processing and are not stored on your device. When you buy credits, an anonymous purchase record is shared with RevenueCat for sales statistics (see Third-Party Services). We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.

How Your Photos Are Used

Your selected clothing and person photos are sent to Replicate servers so the AI model can virtually dress you. The image is deleted by Replicate after processing. Your photos are not shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Face Data

Wearcast does not perform face recognition and does not collect biometric identifiers or face templates. The photos you select may contain a face; the app processes them only as ordinary images to generate the try-on result you request. Face data contained in your photos is: (1) used solely to generate that single virtual try-on image; (2) transmitted securely only to our AI processing provider, Replicate (replicate.com), for that one-time generation; (3) never stored by Wearcast — neither the app nor our proxy server keeps any copy, and Replicate processes the images transiently and deletes them in accordance with its privacy policy, with generated output files expiring automatically within approximately one hour; (4) never used for identification, tracking, advertising, analytics, or AI model training, and never shared with any other party.

Third-Party Services

Replicate (replicate.com) processes your photos to generate the try-on image, using Google's Nano Banana image model. See replicate.com/privacy.

RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) receives a record of each in-app purchase — product, price, currency, date and a random app identifier generated on your device — so we can see sales statistics. It does not receive your name, email address or photos, and the data is not used for advertising or tracking. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple App Attest: to protect our service from abuse, the app uses Apple's App Attest to prove that requests come from a genuine copy of Wearcast on a real device. This uses a cryptographic key created on your device and contains no personal data.

Credit System

Credits you purchase are stored locally on your device. Payments are processed via the Apple App Store and subject to Apple's privacy policy. A purchase record is shared with RevenueCat as described above.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at users under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from this age group.

Contact

For questions about our privacy policy: destek@tryiton.app
